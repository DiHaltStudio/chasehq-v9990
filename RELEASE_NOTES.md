# Chase H.Q. for MSX + V9990 — 1.0

An unofficial homage port of Ocean's 1989 MSX game for an MSX with 64 KB RAM and a Yamaha V9990 cartridge.

The 1 MiB ASCII16 MegaROM includes five chase stages, editable V9990 graphics, PSG music and effects, a new title screen and an ending. Nancy's briefing features a repeating Morse SOS. Impacts with the criminal's car use a longer crash effect.

Download `chasehq_v9990.rom` to play, or `chasehq-v9990-1.0.zip` for the ROM and the English and Spanish manuals. The source repository can build the ROM from both sides of the original MSX cassette supplied separately by the user.

*Chase H.Q.* and its original content belong to their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial port by DiHalt Studio for study, preservation and homage.
